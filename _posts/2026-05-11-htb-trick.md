**HTB Trick** is a Linux machine focused on `DNS`, `WEB` and `SMTP` enumeration. I first discovered the main domain and hidden vhosts through zone transfer and subdomain fuzzing, then exploited an `SQL injection` on the payroll site to dump credentials and a `local file inclusion` on the marketing site to read sensitive files, including michael’s `SSH private key`. After logging in as `michael`, I escalated to root by abusing a misconfigured `fail2ban` setup where group-controlled files let me execute commands as `root` through a service restart.

### Abusing fail2ban
- [fail2ban](https://grumpygeekwrites.wordpress.com/2021/01/29/privilege-escalation-via-fail2ban/)
- Editing `/etc/fail2ban/action.d/iptables-multiport.conf` --> `actionban`
```
michael@trick:/etc/fail2ban/action.d$ mv iptables-multiport.conf iptables-multiport.conf.bak
michael@trick:/etc/fail2ban/action.d$ cp iptables-multiport.conf.bak iptables-multiport.conf
michael@trick:/etc/fail2ban/action.d$ nano iptables-multiport.conf
michael@trick:/etc/fail2ban/action.d$ sudo /etc/init.d/fail2ban restart
```
